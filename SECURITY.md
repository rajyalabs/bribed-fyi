# Security

bribed.fyi handles anonymous reports about corruption. Reporter safety depends on the site
holding as little data as possible and masking what it publishes. If you find a way to
identify a reporter, bypass the masking, read or alter data you shouldn't, or otherwise
put users at risk, please tell us privately first.

- Email: **contact@bribed.fyi**
- Please do not open a public issue for security problems.
- Tell us what you found, how to reproduce it, and what you think the impact is.
- We will acknowledge within a few days, fix what we can quickly, and credit you if you want.

What is in scope: the site at bribed.fyi, its API (`/api/*`), the masking in
`lib/server/redact.ts`, and anything in this repository.

What we already state publicly about data handling: https://bribed.fyi/privacy
